How an order runs

Four steps from the first message to the truck.

  1. Send the area

    Tell us the square feet, the pattern and the thickness you need.

  2. We quote

    Quantity, rate and a delivery date come back in writing.

  3. Cast and cure

    The units are cast in moulds and cured before they are stacked.

  4. Loaded out

    Pallets are loaded and trucked to your site.
