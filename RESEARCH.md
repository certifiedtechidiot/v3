## 2. What they make and sell

IndiaMART describes the business as a manufacturer of paver blocks, manhole covers and compound walls. Its catalogue names:

- Cement Yellow Milano Interlocking Paver Block
- Cement Fan Blade Shape Paver Block
- Grey I Shape Cement Paver Block
- Grey concrete paver block, 80 mm, zig-zag
- Cement paver block, star shape, 60 mm, 9 x 4 inch (TradeIndia)
- RCC manhole cover, 12 x 12 inch, grey, square, lockable
- Cement chequered parking tile
- Precast compound wall and wall compound grill
- Cobbles, pebbles and pavings
- AAC blocks, supplied rather than cast (TradeIndia lists the business as a supplier/trading company for AAC blocks)

Commercial terms in the public listing:

- Listed rate: 50 rupees per piece for the 60 mm star paver
- Minimum order: 400 pieces
- Listed supply: 4,000 pieces per week
- Delivery: about one week
- Payment: cash against delivery or cheque

Justdial also files a "Roshni Enterprises" in Goa under interlocking paver tiles, precast compound walls and cement pipes. The spelling differs, so treat cement pipes as an unconfirmed line until the owner confirms it.

## 3. How they work

The pattern the listings describe is a yard-to-site trade rather than retail:

1. A builder, contractor or owner asks for a quantity and a pattern.
2. The yard quotes per piece or per square foot, with a minimum run of a few hundred pieces.
3. Units are cast and cured at Sancoale, then loaded.
4. Delivery goes out by truck, quoted at about a week, across Goa.

Nothing published confirms whether they lay pavers, whether they deliver outside Goa, or what their installed rates are. The website therefore talks about supply and delivery only, and the enquiry form asks for area, pattern and site.

## 4. Who they sell to

Goa's paver demand comes from villas and residential plots, hotel and resort grounds, colony roads and footpaths, parking bays, and boundary walls. The published listings put the business alongside other Goa precast units rather than in a retail showroom, so the site addresses builders, contractors and owners, and offers a yard visit for samples.

## 5. Channels and gaps

- No public phone, email or street address could be verified. TradeIndia shows "Flat No. 004, Third Floor, Pundalik Arcade, Bepqegal, Goa, 403601", which does not match Sancoale and may belong to a different record.
- No product photographs, client names, certifications or project counts are published. The website uses no invented number, quote, logo or project.

## 6. What this means for the website

- Facts used on the page: established 2016, Sancoale, team of 26 to 50, open Monday to Sunday, six product lines, listed supply of 4,000 pieces a week, the 50 rupee / 400 piece listing.
- Facts left off the page until the owner supplies them: phone, WhatsApp number, email, street address, installed rates, project list, GST and certification claims.
- The enquiry form hands the written enquiry to WhatsApp. Set `SITE.whatsapp` in `assets/js/main.js` to the yard's number and it will address the message directly.
- Photographs are stand-ins from Wikimedia Commons, credited in the footer and marked as slots (`data-slot`) so the owner's own yard and product photos can replace them without touching layout.
